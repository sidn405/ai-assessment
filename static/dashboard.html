🏆 Reading Rewards

Achieve 365 turns your reading practice into progress you can see. Earn points, unlock badges, and level up as you build strong habits — it’s designed to keep you motivated and consistent.

⭐ Points
  • +10 each completed lesson
  • +15 good score
  • +25 high score
  • +50 perfect score
  • +5 daily streak bonus
  • +100 weekly goal completed
  • +20 each badge earned
🎖 Badges

Badges celebrate milestones like your first lesson, perfect streaks, and consistency. Each badge also awards bonus points.

Tip: Check the badges list in this page to see what you’re close to unlocking.

📈 Levels

Your level grows as your total points increase. Levels help you track long-term improvement and reward consistent learning over time.

Level up every 500 total points.

Why this helps
The rewards system keeps you engaged, encourages steady practice, and makes your growth measurable — so you can build reading confidence step-by-step.
